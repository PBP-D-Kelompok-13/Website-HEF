How it works

• Add ingredients and amounts. Spoonacular estimates nutrition per serving from these details.

• Analysis can return a health score (0–100) and dietary labels when available.

• Health score describes nutrition, not environmental impact or carbon footprint.

• Your analyzed recipe is saved under “My recipes”, separate from Spoonacular recipes.
